What does RTP actually mean for you?

RTP is the share of all money wagered on a slot that it's built to pay back over millions of spins. Most online slots sit around 96%. Every slot on this list pushes past 99%, so the house edge is under 1%.

The fine print: RTP is a long-run average, not a promise for one session. A 99% slot won't hand back $99 every time you play $100. It just means the maths is about as player-friendly as slots get.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online gambling legal in Alberta in 2026?
Yes - Alberta launched a fully regulated, multi-operator iGaming market on July 13, 2026. The Alberta Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis Commission (AGLC) licenses operators, and the Alberta iGaming Corporation (AiGC) oversees the market. All five casinos on this page are fully licensed and legal for Alberta players to use.
What is the minimum gambling age in Alberta?
18 years old. This applies to both land-based and online gambling in Alberta - making it more accessible than Ontario, where the legal age is 19. All licensed platforms are required to verify age before allowing deposits.
Do I have to pay tax on online casino winnings in Alberta?
No. In Canada, casino and sports betting winnings are generally not considered taxable income for recreational players. The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) treats gambling as a leisure activity, not a business. The exception is if gambling is your primary income source - in that case, consult a tax professional.

What's the difference between an online casino and a sportsbook?
An online casino offers digital gambling games - slots, blackjack, roulette, baccarat, poker, and live dealer games. A sportsbook lets you bet on the outcomes of real sporting events (hockey, football, soccer, etc.). Many Alberta platforms - including BetMGM and DraftKings - offer both under a single account and shared wallet.
What is Polymarket, and can I use it in Alberta?
Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market platform where users bet on real-world event outcomes (elections, economic data, sports results) using cryptocurrency. It is not an online casino or sportsbook and is not licensed under Alberta's AiGC framework. Canadian users should check current eligibility and regulatory status before participating, as the rules for prediction markets differ from licensed gambling platforms.

Are there responsible gambling tools available?
Yes - and they're mandatory. All AiGC-licensed operators must provide deposit limits, loss limits, session time limits, cool-off periods (temporary breaks), and self-exclusion options.
